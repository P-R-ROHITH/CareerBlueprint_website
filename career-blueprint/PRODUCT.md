# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router), TypeScript strict, Tailwind CSS, Motion for React (`motion/react`), lucide-react, next/image. Pinned by the owner's brief. Deploy target: Vercel or another host permitting commercial use.

## Users

Students, freshers and working professionals in India preparing for jobs: interviews, resumes, Class 12 (NIOS) and personal development. They reach the site mainly on phones and decide by contacting the business on WhatsApp or phone.

## Product Purpose

Career Blueprint is a career guidance and training business. The website explains the services and moves a visitor to a WhatsApp or phone enquiry. Success is a visitor who understands what is offered and makes contact.

## Positioning

Tagline: "The Blueprint to Your Dream Job." Practical, focused guidance rather than placement promises.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Six real routes: `/`, `/programs`, `/programs/interview-training`, `/programs/resume-preparation`, `/about`, `/contact`.
- Four services: Interview Training, NIOS Coaching (Class 12), Resume Preparation, Personality Development. NIOS and Personality Development are enquiry-only, no dedicated pages.
- No contact form, CRM, database or booking backend.
- Contact buttons use only confirmed destinations (WhatsApp and phone), kept in one config file.
- Undecided / owner decisions: WhatsApp and phone numbers (owner is supplying), email, address, team and founder details, service deliverables, durations, pricing, final photography.
- The site does NOT claim years of experience (owner chose to leave it out for now).

## Brand Commitments

Name: Career Blueprint. Logo supplied (CB monogram with figure climbing stairs to a door; navy and blue with a gold door frame; serif wordmark). Palette: navy #102A56, royal blue #2563EB, light blue #EAF2FF, gold #D4A843, white, dark grey #263238, light grey #F5F7FA. Navy primary, royal blue for interaction, gold as restrained emphasis. Serif display plus clean sans body. Aesthetic pinned by the brief: editorial premium, cinematic photography, restrained purposeful motion.

## Evidence on Hand

Logo PNG only. No testimonials, statistics, placements, pricing, durations, founder bio, or customer outcomes exist and none may be invented. Photography will be licensed stock, listed for replacement.

## Product Principles

1. Say only what the owner has confirmed.
2. Every page leads to a WhatsApp or phone enquiry.
3. Mobile phone first, fast, and fully usable without animation.
4. Credibility through restraint, not claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; keyboard focus visible; touch targets and contrast suitable for phone use.
